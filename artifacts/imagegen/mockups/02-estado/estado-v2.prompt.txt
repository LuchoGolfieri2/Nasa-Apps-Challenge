Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: high-fidelity desktop web app screen mockup (1920x1080), second screen of the "Field Rewind" app, used as visual reference before coding.
Input images: Image 1 is the CURRENT approved first screen of the same app (globe landing). Match it exactly in style: same dark background Verde noche #0F2A1D with faint stars, same soft green glow language, same header (logo top-left at 72px margin, same search pill top-right), same typefaces, same button and chip shapes, same spacing rhythm. This screen must feel like the camera flew down from that globe into Kansas. Image 2 is the brand board: use only its colors, logo and typefaces.
Product context: US farmers pick their county, then rewind 10 years of NASA and USDA data. On this screen the camera has flown into the state of Kansas and the farmer picks a county.
Map: the state of Kansas, accurately shaped (a wide rectangle with the notched north-east corner along the Missouri River), divided into its 105 counties in their real rectangular grid layout. Counties filled with a single-hue sequential green ramp from dark muted green (low) to bright Verde brote #8ED081 (high), meaning "share of planted acres in corn"; western counties lower, eastern and south-central higher. 3 or 4 counties have NO data: they show a diagonal hatch pattern in muted gray-green, never empty or black. Thin dark county borders. Neighboring states only as dim outlines without fill.
Selected county: Sumner County, on the southern border of Kansas, south-central, directly south of Sedgwick County (Wichita). It has a thick Oro trigo #E9B949 outline plus a soft gold glow, so selection is shown by outline AND label, not by color alone.
Typography: Bricolage Grotesque for titles, DM Sans for everything else, tabular numerals for all numbers, sentence case everywhere, at most 4 text sizes on screen, typographic punctuation (· and –).
Exact UI text (verbatim, spelled exactly, nothing else): "Field Rewind"; "Search a county or place"; breadcrumb "Globe › Kansas"; county card title "Sumner County, Kansas"; card value "18%" with label "of planted acres in corn, 2025"; card source chip "USDA NASS"; primary button "See its history"; legend title "Share of planted acres in corn"; legend tick labels "0%" "15%" "30%" "45%" "60%+"; legend item "Missing data"; legend source "Source: USDA NASS, 2025"; footer note "Results are for the whole county, not for a single field."; small stamp "Prototype data"; tiny corner label "AI-generated".
Design principles to apply (strictly):
- One primary action per view: only "See its history" is a filled Verde campo #2F7D4F pill with Papel #F6F1E4 text; everything else is outline or text.
- One color, one meaning: green ramp = data only; gold = selection only; no red anywhere; Papel for text.
- Never rely on color alone: missing data uses hatch + label; the selected county uses gold outline + card.
- Group with space, not lines; align everything to the same 72px outer margins as Image 1; order by importance: map first, then the selected county card, then legend, then footnote.
- The map is content and bleeds large; controls float above it with clear insets.
- Shadows for elevation only on the floating card; thin 1.5px Papel borders at low opacity for structure (as in Image 1's chip and search). Nested corner radii consistent (outer radius = inner radius + padding).
- AA contrast for all text, large hit areas (min 48px tall buttons), plain words, verb-first button label.
- Calm, premium, uncluttered, generous negative space, same cinematic glow as Image 1 but restrained.
Avoid: NASA logo or meatball, any text not listed above, lorem ipsum, browser chrome, mouse cursor, dashboards with many charts, rainbow or multi-hue map colors, red, purple, blue, neon, drop shadows on text, more than one filled button.
Composition (variation 2, side panel): map on the left two thirds, Kansas centered there. A fixed right panel 480px wide, slightly lighter dark green with a subtle left border, full height under the header, containing in order: breadcrumb, county title, the big "18%" value with its label, the source chip, the primary button, then the legend (vertical swatches) and the footnote at the bottom of the panel.
